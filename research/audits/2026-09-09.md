The earlier article incorrectly located the mythology's invention in *The Call of Cthulhu* (1928). Removed that origin claim; the letters substantiate the narrower, relevant point.
- **Episode 3 attribution:** Claims about Bigfoot, UAP identities, energy requirements for manifestation, blackness as a physical energy law, a supernatural explanation of sleep paralysis, body modification and named pagan deities have been narrowed or explicitly attributed to the presenter. The earlier draft sometimes stated them in the encyclopedia's voice as established facts.
