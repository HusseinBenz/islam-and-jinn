## Evidence and adaptation decisions

### Modern arguments and attachment

RAND and Patrick Haenni were checked through publisher/author pages. Their existence does not prove RAND directed Khawatir or the motives attributed to presenters. Political allegations, Muhammad Abduh/Mu'tazila generalizations, the unnamed Facebook meme page and earlier unlocated channel/book references are tracked but condensed or omitted.

Ibn Taymiyyah supports attribution of an older scholarly attachment concept, not modern prevalence or a reader's diagnosis. The raqi friend's dream, the widow's experiences and other women's accounts remain anonymous testimony. Heavy limbs, dreams or relationship trouble are not diagnostic signs. Energy costs, qarin spying and manifestation mechanisms have no inspected experiment here.

### Intimacy and law

At **20:29**, the presenter says he has no personal account confirming completed physical intercourse. Preserve that qualification. Claims about protection among Muslim women who contacted him do not establish immunity or imply blame for assault.

Bukhari 141 does not prove jinn parentage when a prayer is omitted. Bukhari 2311 and 247 and Muslim 780 do not validate the forty-day recording/oil protocol around 26:53; it is not offered as a proven treatment.

Qur'an 17:64 is not decisive biological evidence in the presenter's account; Ibn Kathir's commentary is explicitly editorial context. Ibn al-Jawzi's inference from Qur'an 55:56 was inspected at Zad al-masir 8:122; applying Paradise descriptions to earthly unions remains interpretation. Al-Ashqar was read, but his citations of Malik, early authorities and Ahmad remain original-source tasks. Four legal objections and Qur'an 16:72/30:21 are recorded without inventing consensus.

### Offspring

The presenter withholds proof and considers offspring unlikely. Abu Dawud 5107 has al-Albani's displayed weak-chain grading; the report is not verified hybrid biology. Dhu al-Thudayyah's parentage story is separate from sound Nahrawan reports; physical differences do not establish jinn ancestry.

Ibn Kathir critiques an embellished Bilqis narrative. Al-Qurtubi quotes al-Mawardi's objection and then disagrees: the episode's generalization about all commentators rejecting these stories is too broad. Original al-Mawardi, al-Tabari and individual chains remain unfinished.

Abu Dawud 4403 distinguishes sleeping-person accountability from deliberate conduct. The unlocated pre-Flood/Theosophy theory is omitted. Only the opening section of Malleus Part I, Question III was inspected: it proposes human semen transfer, not verified chromosome modification or hybrid biology. The rest of the chapter was not read. IVF/chromosome analogies are not a named controlled experiment or universal biological law.

### Stories and films

Hearn's Yuki-onna, the BnF Melusine record, Sundance's Rodney Ascher Q&A, AFI's The Entity record and original distributor synopses support limited literary/film facts, not supernatural causation. Zeus equated with Iblis is the presenter's interpretation. Cambion, kitsune and Welsh variants need original references.

The Nightmare presents accounts and reconstructions. The director's stated uncertainty concerns their origin, not proof of his religion or every participant's beliefs. Chris, Connie and an anonymous assault account need original timestamps and identities; do not merge them. Doris Bither's case records were not inspected. AFI records The Entity's US release as 4 February 1983, while the episode calls it 1982; check production/territorial dates. Its proposed 2025 remake is unverified. Satan's Mistress has only identification leads.

The Black Room's distributor describes the husband's possession. Rosemary's Baby's fictional child is not simply the Islamic Dajjal. An uncheckable third-hand suicide account is omitted; fiction does not establish prognosis. NHS sleep-paralysis information is explicitly added editorial context, not a diagnosis of the reported people.
