## Translation and source corrections

- Around 07:30, the auto-captions corrupt a proposed number of years before Adam. Do not restore a number from imagination.
- 07:53–08:10: the forty-year clay story is explicitly treated as weak/Israelite. It is not part of Muslim 2611a. Ibn Kathir, Ahmad Shakir and al-Tabari references remain separate checks.
- 08:32–09:10: distinguish the weak/mursal three-things reports from the four-things statement attributed to Ibn Umar. Al-Tabari's text confirms the latter is mawquf; it does not itself verify every grading attribution made in the video.
- 09:44–10:16: angels' initial state versus righteous humans' final reward is attributed to Ibn Taymiyyah. Exact original locator remains open.
- 11:25–12:48: the presenter critiques elemental temperament reasoning. Ibn Kathir's inspected 7:12 commentary actually uses such reasoning. Preserve the disagreement.
- 17:39–18:24: al-Ashqar quotes Majmu al-fatawa 11:309. The first-person speaker in the quotation is the reporting shaykh, not automatically Ibn Taymiyyah. The captions/presenter's gloss needs checking against the original context.
- 19:04–19:23: Muslim 2175a concerns averting suspicion; the proposed connection to acupuncture meridians is the presenter's hypothesis.
- 20:01: Muslim 2236a says three days. Do not silently turn the abbreviated caption into a practical rule covering every snake.
- 20:11: Suraqah-at-Badr is called sound by the speaker; its original report and grade are not yet verified.
- 35:11–35:47: Qur'an 27:40 names neither Asif nor a physical speed. Retain the distinction between the text and the speaker's explanations.
- 38:55: check the named minority authorities separately. Al-Ashqar records al-Dahhak, Ibn Hazm and Ibn al-Jawzi; al-Juwayni was not found in the inspected section.
- 42:16: dream teaching is explicitly introduced as the speaker's theory, not a transmitted prophetic event.
- 43:29: the exact sea quotation belongs to 45:12, despite the spoken/caption association with Ibrahim. The sun/moon quotation is 14:33. Confirm the audio slip.
