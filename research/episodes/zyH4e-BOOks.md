The following source identifications are inherited from the earlier pass and are **not newly verified** by the 21 September translation. Original audio and editions remain necessary:

- 02:48: Warning against seeking religious knowledge for contention or fame: identified as Jami' al-Tirmidhi 2654 and Sunan Ibn Majah 253/254; graded hasan/sahih lighayrihi by al-Albani (`hadith-seeking-knowledge`).
- 03:07–03:19: Creation from smokeless flame of fire (*marij min nar*): Qur'an 55:15 (`quran-55-15`) and Sahih Muslim 2996 (`muslim-2996`).
- 04:22: Ibn Abi al-Dunya's *Hawatif al-Jinan* cited regarding reports of jinn funerals. Noted as an anecdotal compilation of historical reports, not theological proof (`e02-ibn-abi-dunya-hawatif`).
- 06:00: The view that Iblis is the progenitor of all jinn is widely reported from early authorities (Ibn Abbas, al-Hasan, Mujahid, Qatadah) and chosen by Ibn Taymiyyah in *Majmu' al-Fatawa* 4/235 (`e02-scholars-iblis-father`). The presenter advances three textual and theological arguments against this view, preferring the position that Iblis was an individual member of a pre-existing created species.
- 09:50: The *Hinn* and *Binn* pre-Adamic creatures in Tabari's *Tarikh* and Ibn Kathir's *al-Bidayah* (`e02-hinn-binn`). Presenter explicitly notes this lacks verified scriptural evidence.
- 10:46: Narration of al-Dahhak from Ibn Abbas that devils are the children of Iblis who die only with him: recorded in *Tafsir al-Tabari* 18:50 (#23772) (`e02-dahhak-ibn-abbas`).
- 11:17: The tradition naming *Sumya* or *al-Jann* as the father of jinn: recorded in *Tafsir al-Baghawi* 1/76 and *al-Bidayah wa al-Nihayah* 1/84 (`e02-sumya-father`).
- 12:01: The authentic hadith classifying jinn into three categories (flying with wings, serpents and dogs, and nomadic dwellers): recorded in al-Hakim's *al-Mustadrak* 2/456, al-Tabarani, and *Sahih al-Jami'* 3114 (`e02-three-classes-jinn`).
- 13:43: Sheikh Ali al-Tantawi's *Ta'rif 'Amm bi Din al-Islam* (pp. 35–37) cited and respectfully critiqued for suggesting devils may have been created without choice (`e02-tantawi-book`).
- 15:07 & 15:58: Western pop culture examples: *Little Nicky* (2000, dir. Steven Brill, starring Adam Sandler) (`e02-little-nicky`) and *Hellboy* (`e02-hellboy`), contrasted with Islamic accounts of moral agency and Hell.
- 17:13: Weak report claiming one-third of jinn were created as devils doomed to punishment: classified by al-Albani as *da'if jiddan* in *Silsilat al-Ahadith al-Da'ifah* #2921 (`e02-weak-hadith-jinn-species`).
- 18:40: Abbas Mahmud al-'Aqqad's *Iblis* cited regarding the ancient linguistic roots of the name across Semitic languages (`e02-aqaad-iblis`).
- 20:20–24:10: The *qarin* (companion): scripturally established in Qur'an 50:23, 50:27, 43:36, and Sahih Muslim 2814a/2815. Explained not as a coerced demonic creation, but as an assigned devil who voluntarily chose rebellion and whom Allah permits to test human beings (Qur'an 4:90, 47:4).
