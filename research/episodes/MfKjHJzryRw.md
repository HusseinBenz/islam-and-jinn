## Translation and ASR questions

The following is the inherited source map. Its verification claims are provisional and superseded where noted in the 9 September audit:

- 01:35: Authentic hadith on three primary classes of jinn (wings, serpents/dogs, nomadic): verified in al-Mustadrak 2/456 (#3702), Sahih Ibn Hibban 6156, and Sahih al-Jami' 3114 (`e03-three-classes-hadith`).
- 02:09: Qur'an 38:37 ("والشياطين كل بناء وغواص") describing specialized artisan and diving devils (`quran-38-37`).
- 02:40: Sheikh Muhammad al-Hasan Walad al-Dedew televised lecture on 4Shbab TV (*Mfahim* series) comparing jinn diversity to the animal kingdom from ant to elephant, and noting scholars who enumerated up to 460 types (`e03-dedew-mfahim`).
- 05:07: Hadith of the jinn delegation asking for provision for themselves and their riding beasts (*dawabb*): verified in Sahih Muslim 450 (`muslim-450`).
- 08:38 & 08:45: Winged angels in Qur'an 35:1 (`quran-35-1`) and Jibril with 600 wings in Sahih al-Bukhari 3232 (`bukhari-3232`).
- 11:16 & 11:59: UAP witness statements from Dr. Hal Puthoff on *The Diary of a CEO* (`e03-hal-puthoff`) and Dr. Eric Davis reported in the *New York Post* (`e03-erik-davis`) regarding alleged non-human typologies.
- 14:11: Ibn 'Abd al-Barr in *al-Tamhid* 1/345 commenting that *yahilluna wa yaz'anun* denotes the ghul and si'lah (`e03-ibn-abd-al-barr-ghul`).
- 20:35: Qur'an 37:64–65 on the tree of Zaqqum with fruit like heads of devils (`quran-37-64-65`).
- 22:41: Henry Fuseli's 1781 oil painting *The Nightmare* illustrating incubus/sleep-paralysis iconography (`e03-fuseli-nightmare`).
- 23:31–25:12: Debunking the fictional grimoire *al-Azif* (*Necronomicon*) and its fictional author "Abdul Alhazred" invented by American horror writer H. P. Lovecraft in *The Call of Cthulhu* (1928) (`e03-lovecraft-cthulhu`, `e03-azif-necro`).
- 26:39: Black dog hadith in Sahih Muslim 510 (`muslim-510`) and Ibn Taymiyyah's explanation in *Majmu' al-Fatawa* 19/52 (`e03-ibn-taymiyyah-black-dog`).
- 28:54: John Ramirez's *Out of the Devil's Cauldron* on modern occult body modification (`e03-john-ramirez`).
- 30:41: Éliphas Lévi's 1856 drawing of Baphomet (`e03-eliphas-levi-baphomet`), Templar trial origins (`e03-templar-trials`), and Anton LaVey's Church of Satan (`e03-church-of-satan`).
- 33:44 & 33:50: Gustave Doré's *Paradise Lost* engravings (`e03-dore-lucifer`) and Guillaume Geefs' statue *Le génie du mal* in Liège Cathedral (`e03-liege-statue`).
- 37:25–38:00: Umar ibn al-Khattab wrestling a jinn in human form: Sunan al-Darimi 3381/3422, al-Bayhaqi *Dala'il* 7/48, verified as authentic isnad by Ibn Kathir and al-Haythami (`e03-umar-wrestling-jinn`).
- 40:08 & 40:50: The *marid* (rebel) in Qur'an 37:7 (`quran-37-7`) and the *ifrit* in Qur'an 27:39 (`quran-27-39`).
