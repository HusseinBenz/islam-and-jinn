## Translation and ASR questions

The ASR is readable but unsuitable for verbatim religious quotation. The following are **inherited identifications**, not claims of fresh audiovisual review. Primary editions and original recordings still need inspection unless the 18 September audit states otherwise:

- 06:30–07:25: The captions give "Ibrahim al-Ibrashi" and later "Dr. Hisham". Without audio, we cannot determine whether this was a spoken correction or ASR error. The reference is to Dr. Hisham Sidqi al-Ibrashi's field study on West African pagan religions and spirit cults (*al-Diyanat al-Wad'iyyah fi Gharb Ifriqya*); verified.
- 27:56–28:18: Bukhari 461 (Abu Hurayrah) narrates the ifrit interruption without saliva wording. The saliva narration is independently recorded in Sunan Abi Dawud 773 and Musnad Ahmad 11354/11776 from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri ("حتى وجدت برد لعابه/ريقه بين إصبعي هاتين"), graded Sahih by al-Albani and al-Arna'ut; verified in `e01-saliva-variant`.
- 31:34–32:34: The film is *Three Thousand Years of Longing* (2022, dir. George Miller, starring Idris Elba and Tilda Swinton), adapted from the novella *The Djinn in the Nightingale's Eye* (1994) by English author A. S. Byatt. The character takes the djinn to London, UK; verified in `e01-three-thousand-years` and `e01-nightingale`.
- 33:11–33:45: The confined-devils report is *mawquf* on 'Abd Allah ibn 'Amr ibn al-'As in the *Muqaddimah* of Sahih Muslim (report 13). Ibn Baz (fatwa 3291) notes it is likely from Isra'iliyyat and must not be attributed as a prophetic hadith; verified in `e01-muslim-imprisoned`.
- 34:10–34:36: Hamid Adam's interview, broadcaster, and the alleged jinn's name require audiovisual identification.
- 35:11–35:59: ASR garbled Thelema as "salama". Crowley founded Thelema after breaking with the Golden Dawn; his 1919 drawing of Lam (Tibetan for "Way/Path") and commentary on Blavatsky's *The Voice of the Silence* documented in `e01-crowley-lam`.
- 40:03–42:33: Paul Hellyer's 2005 speech and 2014 RT SophieCo interview (`e01-hellyer`), the 2017 NYT AATIP investigation (`e01-nyt-2017`), David Grusch's 2023 congressional testimony, and Rep. Anna Paulina Luna's Joe Rogan appearance. **18 September correction:** Luna appeared in JRE 2365 on 13 August 2025; the old 2024/#2184 locator was wrong. Official clip captions have now been read; support is testimony-only. Other media-event labels in this older paragraph have not all been re-audited.
- 48:00–48:28: The narration of Umar ibn al-Khattab and Abu al-Haytham (*Barid al-Jinn*) bringing early news of military victory to Madinah is recorded in Ibn Abi al-Dunya's *Hawatiff al-Jinan* (#69), Ibn Hajar's *al-Isabah* (7/465), and al-Bayhaqi's *Dala'il al-Nubuwwah* (6/389). The chain has known weaknesses, and the presenter explicitly noted it is unconfirmed; verified in `e01-umar-messenger`.
